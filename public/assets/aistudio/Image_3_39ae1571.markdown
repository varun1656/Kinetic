## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-clean, minimalist, high-contrast visual architecture tailored for on-demand transit and urban mobility. Built upon a direct utilitarian foundation, the UI eliminates visual noise, placing focus exclusively on spatial orientation, destination selection, and vehicle status.

### Aesthetic Movement & Philosophy
The aesthetic blends **Modern Swiss Structuralism** with **High-Contrast Utilitarianism**:
- **Absolute Contrast:** Deep jet black anchors interactive elements directly against pure, luminous white planes, ensuring immediate recognition in variable outdoor lighting conditions.
- **Architectural Precision:** Razor-sharp surfaces, micro-borders, and strict geometric alignment eliminate decorative fluff, reinforcing punctuality, velocity, and trust.
- **Dynamic Clarity:** Information hierarchy relies strictly on scale, font weight, and structural spacing rather than decorative colors or heavy elevation effects.

## Colors

The palette is resolutely monochromatic, using surgical splashes of functional color purely for wayfinding, telemetry, and critical transaction confirmation.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#000000`):** Jet Black. Applied to critical actions, dominant typography, selected ride tier cards, and primary map vectors.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure White. The primary ground for sheets, dialogs, card tiles, and mobile app chrome.
- **Subtle Neutrals:**
  - `Surface Subdued (#F6F6F6)`: Field fills, unselected ride option backgrounds, card hovers.
  - `Border Soft (#E2E2E2)`: Clean hairline boundaries between UI layers and card perimeters.
  - `Neutral Muted (#767676)`: Supporting metadata, timestamps, distance metrics, and inactive glyphs.
- **Semantic Accents:**
  - `Secondary (`#276EF1`)`: Navigation route polyline, live driver GPS beacon, active map pick-up pins.
  - `Tertiary / Success (`#05A357`)`: Pricing drops, safety confirmation badges, active discount toggles.
  - `Destructive (`#E11900`)`: Ride cancellation alerts, expired payment notifications.

### Dark Mode Adaptability
In reversed contexts, the roles strictly invert: canvas becomes `#000000`, elevated sheets sit at `#121212`, card surfaces rest at `#1A1A1A`, and primary actions render in `#FFFFFF` with `#000000` label text.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes **Inter** for all display, body, and interface markers. Its tall x-height, neutral geometric construction, and ultra-crisp horizontal metrics mimic bespoke architectural wayfinding systems.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Used for prominent prompt screens ("Where to?", "Confirm your pickup spot"). Tight negative tracking conveys modern velocity.
- **Section & Product Titles (`headline-md`, `headline-sm`):** Reserved for vehicle service tiers (e.g., Comfort, Black, Green) and receipt totals.
- **Body:** Standardized across trip summaries, driver instructions, and legal policies. Regular 400 weight ensures effortless legibility over moving map canvas backgrounds.
- **Labels:** Semibold weights for touch targets, ETA counters, badge chips, and license plate tags. License plates and verification codes should apply uppercase transforms with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a hybrid structure: a fluid map stage foundation overlaid by strict structural floating panels and anchored sheets.

### Grid & Responsiveness
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Fixed floating left-hand rail (width: 420px) containing booking flows, location inputs, vehicle selection, and pricing, floating over full-bleed interactive map canvas. Canvas padding rests at `margin: 2rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Bottom-sheet architecture anchored to screen edges with `margin-mobile: 1rem` floating utility buttons (GPS recenter, SOS, Safety Hub). The bottom sheet expands through gesture-based snap points (collapsed peek at 140px, middle tier at 50vh, full screen at 90vh).

### Vertical Rhythm
A rigid 4px/8px incremental rhythm is enforced. Touch surfaces strictly respect a minimum 48px target zone, while structural vertical gaps between input fields and action bars standardise to `space-md` (16px).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids soft multi-layered blur shadows or glowing gradients. Depth is established through structural surface tiering, high-contrast borders, and focused ambient occlusion.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Base Level (Level 0 - Ground):** Live interactive map layer styled in desaturated silver-gray scales with pure black street names and slate-blue route lines.
2. **Sheet Surface (Level 1 - Floating Containers):** White (`#FFFFFF`) modular panels framed with a crisp hairline border (`1px solid #E2E2E2`). For floating desktop cards, a single crisp ambient shadow is permitted: `0px 4px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
3. **Selected Elements (Level 2 - Interactivity):** Active vehicle tier items switch to a black perimeter (`2px solid #000000`), immediately surfacing the card without relying on vertical lift.
4. **Modal & Urgent Drawers (Level 3):** System alerts and driver arrival popovers sit over a 40% alpha jet-black scrim (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`), flanked by a dense ground shadow: `0px 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (`1`)** geometry. UI components favor architectural, restrained curves rather than playful pill structures, reinforcing precision and speed.

### Geometry Token Rules
- **Base Components (Inputs, Vehicle Cards, Buttons):** Standard radius of `0.25rem` (4px). Provides subtle refinement while maintaining sharp visual discipline.
- **Card Groups & Modular Sheets (`rounded-lg`):** Outer sheet top edges and desktop floating cards utilize `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Exceptions (Pills & Discs):** Map floating action icons (e.g., crosshair locate, back button) and status badges (e.g., "Fastest", "Top Rated") use circular or pill geometry to contrast against the sharp rectangular content cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#000000` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Flat, 0px border, `roundedness: 1` (4px). Hover state transitions to `#262626`. Height: 52px for primary mobile actions, 44px for secondary workflows.
- **Secondary:** Solid `#EEEEEE` background with `#000000` text. Hover transitions to `#E2E2E2`.
- **Outline / Ghost:** Transparent background with `1px solid #E2E2E2` border and `#000000` text.
- **Destructive:** Transparent or subtle red-tint ground (`#FDF2F2`) with `#E11900` text.

### Location Input Fields
- Enclosed in a stacked vertical container connected by a visual trip line (a tiny 6px black square for pickup, a 6px black dot for destination, connected by a 1px dashed `#767676` thread).
- Field ground: `#F6F6F6` with zero border in resting state; switches to crisp `#000000` 1px outline on focus.
- Trailing actions: Clear (X) icon button and "Add stop (+)" pill trigger.

### Ride Selection Cards
- Horizontal card structure displaying vehicle graphic, service tier title, passenger count indicator, ETA, and final dynamic price.
- **Resting:** Flat white surface with subtle 1px divider lines separating items.
- **Hover:** Background shifts to `#F9F9F9`.
- **Selected:** Framed with an unequivocal `2px solid #000000` border, subtly set back with a 2px interior offset margin.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact markers for ride attributes ("Fastest", "Electric", "Luggage x2").
- Height: 24px, 4px corner radius, background `#F3F3F3`, text `#000000` in `label-sm`.
- Accent chip (Promotion / Discount): `#E6F4EA` background with `#05A357` text.

### Route Map Elements
- **Polyline:** Heavy 4px solid line in `#000000` for primary completed/planned trajectory, `#276EF1` for active live vehicle segment.
- **Pins:** Minimalist black circular discs with high-contrast centered white icon markers; driver vehicle represented by an oriented monochrome vehicle glyph.
